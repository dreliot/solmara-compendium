title: Solmara Compendium
---
The trail is long and your lips are chapped dry. When you pause the only sound is the rustling of the live oaks and the shek-shek-shek of Steller's jays screaming overhead. It's one of those days that's so hot, you're pressed between two suns as the heat radiates back up out of the hard clay earth. Your feet sweat in your boots. It’s still a week to Ossadavia, to shade, and real rest in a real bed. You can almost see Fink's tea stall, the condensation on the outside of your cold glass, honey drifting in a lazy whirlpool as you stir your spoon. 

The wind blows in from the coast and you think for a second you smell smoke, but you don’t see anything on the horizon. Is your mind playing tricks again? Burn Season is still months away. The Grove Cooperative's Greenkeepers are trying to hold the line but they're stretched thin; few communities would survive without their drought-tolerant seed varietals. More precious than coin.

Solmara was once a powerful nation, now it's a spiderweb of trail-connected communities in flammable forests, manzanita-dotted chaparral, and cracked desert. Mistgate to Sana Amaris, generations after massive wildfires burned the old world to the ground, life persists in a carefully-managed cycle of burn and renewal. Arcane Sunstone, mined from beneath the earth, turns sunlight into power a town can live on. Solmara is trying again, maybe differently this time.

But Burn is coming early this year, like it has the last several years. And some of the fires smell strange. Rumors whisper of a flame unquenchable by water. No one wants to say the word, but its vibrations still hang in the blistering air: *arson*.
