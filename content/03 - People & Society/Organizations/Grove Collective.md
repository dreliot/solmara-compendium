## Grove Collective: FAQ

### 1. Why do the Greenkeepers exist?

- What happened in Solmara that made The Grove Collective organization necessary? 
	 The Grove Collective was founded as a community outreach arm of Ossadavia University. Ossadavia University, known for it's incredible innovation in water conservation technology, animals husbandry, and farming, joined with local community leaders, city councils, and farmers associations. The agreement initially included mutual support, farmers providing testing environments for new equipments, seeds, and technology for Ossadavia. During and after the Ashfall Years, the Grove Collective's role expanded significantly when the Solmara Federal Greenkeepers and the Grove Collective joined forces to create fire breaks, patrol trails, and maintain forests. Eventually, the High Solmaran Council granted the Grove Collective exclusive control over the Greenkeepers, they're now the main face of the Grove Collective. The Grove Collective essentially is Ossadavia University at this point.

- Were they founded in response to the Ashfall, or did something like them exist before it?
	 They weren't founded in response to the Ashfall Years but they were irrevocably changed by them, expanding massively in power and influence such that in many places with no reigonal governments, the Greenkeepers function as something like ecological sheriffs.

- What problem would go unsolved if the Greenkeepers disappeared tomorrow?
	 If the Grove Collective disappeared, there would be a massive disruption to food distribution, energy production, and Solmaraan ability to continue to farm in an increasingly warm and dry environment. The drought-resistant and fire-resistant seed varietals they've created literally saved Solmara.
	 
	 The Greenkeepers themselves are like trail wardens. They make large cirecles (trailtracks) where they serve as like field biologists meets park rangers. They help maintain roads and trails between communities, they check on soil conditions and animal life, they plant new trees and clear fire breaks, they even help communities do controlled burns. But just by the nature of their circles, they help spread news and make connections between communities too. And they serve as liaisons between the communities they serve and the larger Grove Collective organization in Ossadavia.

- What does a Greenkeeper believe they're protecting: the land, the people who live on it, the future, or some combination?

### 2. What does a Greenkeeper actually do all day?

Imagine following one Greenkeeper through an ordinary week.

- What practical skills do they need? What might they be surprisingly bad at?
    
- How much of their work is ecological management, and how much is caring for people?
    
- What does a Greenkeeper carry when traveling between communities?
    
- What does a Greenkeeper's arrival mean to a small town? Relief? Extra work? An argument about water?
    
- What work happens quietly, far from the public eye, that keeps communities alive?
    

### 3. What makes them an organization rather than just skilled individuals?

- How does someone become a Greenkeeper? Apprenticeship, formal education, family tradition, something else?
    
- Are they organized into local posts, regional districts, traveling crews, or something messier?
    
- How much authority does a senior Greenkeeper have over a junior one?
    
- What knowledge is shared freely, and what is guarded?
    
- If two Greenkeepers disagree about what a landscape needs, who decides what happens?
    

### 4. Why do people trust them—and resent them?

- What can a Greenkeeper provide that a community cannot easily produce for itself?
    
- What decisions do Greenkeepers make that affect other people's livelihoods?
    
- Have they ever protected an ecosystem at the expense of the people living there?
    
- Can a community refuse their advice or intervention?
    
- What does a Greenkeeper do when local needs conflict with what they believe the land can sustain?
    

### 5. What are their limits?

- What problems are beyond their ability to solve?
    
- What resources are scarce even for them?
    
- Do they have political authority, or do people mostly cooperate because they need them?
    
- Who can challenge their decisions? Who should be able to?
    
- What happens when a Greenkeeper makes a mistake?
    

### 6. What does the organization feel like from the inside?

This is the section that can keep them from feeling like a generic fantasy order.

- What makes someone proud to be a Greenkeeper?
    
- What do Greenkeepers complain about over dinner?
    
- What are the internal disagreements: old guard versus new ideas, local needs versus regional priorities, science versus tradition?
    
- What does the organization do well that outsiders never notice?
    
- What is one thing about being a Greenkeeper that the recruitment pitch would leave out?
    

## A small writing exercise

Don't try to answer all six sections at once. Start with these three sentence stems:

- The Greenkeepers exist because…
    
- People need the Greenkeepers, but…
    
- A good Greenkeeper knows that…
