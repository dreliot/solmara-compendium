[Sana Amaris](geo:32.7157062,-117.1638284)

Oldest of Solmara's cities, founded on the coast. Characterized by a robust culture of trade with the nation to the south, [[Nospatria]], and believed by many to be the place where [[Amaris]] was pulled from the water by [[Cazira]]. 
## Coronda
[Coronda](geo:32.69122383685424,-117.17708587646486)
 Peninsula which shields Sana Amaris Bay from storms, connected to the land by a thin strip, Coronda was relatively unburnt during Ashfall and therefore has some Golden Era architecture and conservative communities full of rich traders.
